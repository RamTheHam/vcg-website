# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Senior European executives — typically 20+ years across PE, operating, and board roles — who have proprietary deal origination potential: they know which company can be bought before the auction begins, they can reach the seller directly, and they have the conviction to run the company for the next five to seven years. They are not looking for a placement. They are looking for a co-principal.

Secondary: PE firm partners who receive inbound from VCG and look Henrik up. They need to see a credible, rigorous counterparty, not a consultant.

The right primary reader recognises themselves immediately and feels named. The wrong reader leaves. Both outcomes are correct.

## Product Purpose

VCG closes the Principal Gap: senior executives generate the majority of PE returns but are compensated as employees, not as principals. Henrik Rammer acts as a co-originator — the executive brings the thesis, VCG brings PE access and deal-structuring, and they split the origination fee 50/50.

The site's job is to generate one thing: a qualified first conversation. Not awareness, not leads, not downloads. A conversation with an executive who has a real deal view and is ready to act on it.

## Brand Personality

Operator-native. Independent. Selective.

The prose density of private intelligence writing — Tortoise Media, The Information, Every. Every sentence is load-bearing; nothing decorates. Authority comes from specificity and track record, not from claim or institutional affiliation. The firm is small by design. Henrik is the brand; the institution is his philosophy made structural.

Content can be richer than minimal — 10–20% more prose than a stripped advisory site — but each addition must earn its place. "Weigh every word in gold" is the editing standard.

## Anti-references

VCG is its own category: executive-led deal origination where PE is one financing option, not the principal. The executive is a co-investor, not a candidate or a client. The site must feel nothing like:

- **Big-4 / McKinsey advisory**: polished, institutional, fee-first, safe. Authority through scale rather than conviction.
- **Executive search / headhunter**: candidate-brokering, placement-focused, the executive as product.
- **PE firm investor relations**: AUM tables, fund vintage language, LP-relations tone.
- **LinkedIn thought-leadership / executive coaching**: "unlock your potential," personal-brand optimisation, carousel logic. The register is fine; the self-promotional machinery is not.

The tell in all four: they explain themselves for an audience that doesn't already know. VCG writes for the person who already understands the problem. If you need the concept explained from scratch, this isn't the site for you — and that is intentional.

## Design Principles

1. **Outcome over activity.** VCG is compensated for results, not process. Every design element should earn its place the same way. If a section, component, or line of copy could be cut without loss, it should be cut.

2. **The person is the institution.** Henrik is the brand. The site speaks in first person where it matters. Credentials are shown (logos, stats, named sources) not asserted. Authority is demonstrated, never claimed.

3. **Selective by design.** Self-selection is a feature, not a failure mode. The right executive reads the hero and thinks "that's me." The wrong one leaves at the second paragraph. Optimising for the latter would compromise the former.

4. **Show the artefact.** The Value Catalyst Plan is made visible. Statistics carry named sources. Economics are specific. Trust is built through specificity — never through superlatives or social proof machinery.

5. **Every word is load-bearing.** A sentence that could appear on any advisory website does not belong here. The editing standard is private intelligence writing: nothing generic, nothing decorative, nothing that wastes the reader's attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. European professional audience, primarily desktop in office or travel contexts. Reduced motion must be honoured (already implemented). Colour contrast on muted text and footer copy needs review against AA — flag any token below 4.5:1 on body text.
